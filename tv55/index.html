One reliability caution

RTINGS reported (updated November 18, 2026) that many older WOLED panels, 2020 to 2023, developed dead pixels along the edges in its heavy-use testing, while the three QD-OLED sets it tested did not (they did show burn-in from a continuous news-channel test). It did not test 2024 or newer panels, it notes industry experts say panels made from 2024 are significantly improved, and it still rates OLED well for durability. Most sets here are WOLED, so consider extended coverage and ask whether the plan includes panel defects. Read the RTINGS study ↗

Method, exclusions and cautions

Prices were read from each retailer's website on Friday, November 27, 2026, about 6:00 to 7:00 PM Central. Holiday pricing changes quickly; confirm the price in the cart before buying.

Reviews. Expert findings are summarized in our own words; follow each link for the full review. Where a model has not been reviewed by an outlet, the report says so. Some reviews tested a different screen size than 55 inches.

Owner ratings are the averages shown on each store's page; some stores pool ratings across screen sizes.

Left out:

  • All Samsung OLEDs, because they run the Tizen home screen, which was ruled out for this purchase. Note that CNET currently names a Samsung S90F its best-value OLED.
  • Sony BRAVIA 6 OLED (about $998): costs about the same as the higher-ranked LG C6.
  • LG C4 (2024) and Sony K-55XR8B (a sister version of the BRAVIA 8): older or near-duplicate choices.
  • A Sansui 55-inch OLED (about $500 at B&H): lesser-known brand that we did not evaluate.
  • Open-box, refurbished, scratch-and-dent and warranty-bundle listings.
