Buying guide

What buying costs on top of the price

Enter a price. The second total applies the proposed 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers (a US citizen buying in his own name would count). It is reported to start July 1, 2027 but is not yet law.

Old Town rules a buyer inherits

  • The Old Town is a protected traditional settlement and many buildings are individually listed. Façade, roof, window and colour changes need approval from the Ephorate of Antiquities of Argolida.
  • Not every house inside the walls is listed. The lawyer should confirm the exact building's status; it decides what can change and whether restoration grants apply.
  • Every sale needs an engineer's certificate that the building has no illegal works, or that they were legalised (Law 4495/2017).
  • Apartments such as the penthouse sit in shared buildings. Read the building rules for limits on short lets, works and shared costs.
  • Cars are restricted in the lanes; parking is by the harbour and the Land Gate.

Golden Visa and renting

  • In Argolida the property route needs one home of at least €400,000 and 120 m². Smaller units cannot be combined. Restoring a formally listed building can qualify at €250,000.
  • A Golden Visa home cannot be rented short-term; in October 2026 the minister warned permits will be revoked for it. Long leases are allowed.
  • Short lets elsewhere need an AMA registration from the tax office and safety equipment (Law 5170/2025). Registration ends when a property is sold.
  • Nafplio is not in the Athens or Thessaloniki short-let freeze zones.

Living on Syntagma Square

  • The square is the Old Town's living room: café tables fill the marble paving from spring to autumn, with music and events into summer nights. Upper floors trade some quiet for the view.
  • A few minutes on foot: the harbour promenade and Bourtzi boats, Arvanitia beach, the 999 steps to Palamidi, the Archaeological Museum and the Old Parliament.
  • Nafplio was the first capital of modern Greece (1829 to 1834). Its Venetian, Ottoman and neoclassical buildings are why so many are listed.
  • About two hours by car from Athens airport; busy with Athenian weekenders all year and tourists April to October.

Checklist before an offer

  • Get a Greek tax number (AFM) and a Greek bank account.
  • Hire an independent lawyer to check title, debts, listed status and permits at the land registry.
  • Commission an engineer for the legality certificate and a structural survey.
  • Compare the price with the state's objective value for the street.
  • Ask agents for closing prices of recent Old Town sales.
  • Time the deed: if the 15% rule passes, the signing date should decide which rate applies.
